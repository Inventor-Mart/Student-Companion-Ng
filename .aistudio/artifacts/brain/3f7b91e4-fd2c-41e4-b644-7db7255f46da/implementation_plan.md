# SS 2 Lesson Notes Curriculum (Batches 1 to 5: Complete 15 Subjects)

Comprehensive 12-week Second Term lesson note curricula for Senior Secondary 2 (SS 2) covering all 15 subjects across Science, Commercial, and Arts streams. Built strictly according to the Nigerian NERDC/WAEC syllabus with custom colored SVG diagrams, rich Markdown comparison tables, worked analytical drills, and zero ASCII art.

---

## Complete Subject Rollout Summary (15 Subjects, 180 Lesson Modules)

### Batch 1: Core Foundation
1. **Mathematics** (`src/data/ss2MathData.ts`): Logarithms, inequalities, circle theorems, trigonometry, bearings, probability, grouped data statistics, ogives.
2. **English Language** (`src/data/ss2EnglishData.ts`): Consonant clusters, complex sentence clauses (noun, adjectival, adverbial), formal letters, argumentative essays, stress patterns, summary writing, concord.
3. **Biology** (`src/data/ss2BiologyData.ts`): Digestion & enzyme action, blood & circulation, plant transport, respiration, nephron & osmoregulation, skeleton & locomotion, reproduction, ecology.

### Batch 2: Pure Sciences & Agronomy
4. **Chemistry** (`src/data/ss2ChemistryData.ts`): Periodic table trends, chemical bonding, rates of reactions, chemical equilibrium (Le Chatelier), water & solubility, acids/bases, carbon & allotropes, organic chemistry (hydrocarbons, alkanols).
5. **Physics** (`src/data/ss2PhysicsData.ts`): Projectile motion, circular motion & SHM, machines & mechanical advantage, thermal expansion & gas laws, heat transfer, wave properties & sound, light optics (mirrors, lenses, refraction).
6. **Agricultural Science** (`src/data/ss2AgricData.ts`): Soil fertility & plant nutrients, agricultural ecology & ecosystems, crop production (cereals, legumes, root crops), farm animals (anatomy, physiology, breeding, nutrition), pasture & range management, agricultural marketing.

### Batch 3: Computing & Social Sciences
7. **Data Processing** (`src/data/ss2DataProcessingData.ts`): Relational database management systems (RDBMS), SQL DDL/DML, spreadsheet automation, web design & HTML/CSS fundamentals, networking topologies, internet safety & cybersecurity.
8. **Economics** (`src/data/ss2EconomicsData.ts`): Price determination & elasticity of demand/supply, theory of production & cost curves, market structures (perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly), money & banking, public finance & national income accounting.
9. **Civic Education** (`src/data/ss2CivicData.ts`): Citizenship & constitutional duties, human rights & rule of law, democracy & electoral processes, national integrity & anti-corruption, combating cultism & drug abuse, peacebuilding.

### Batch 4: Applied Sciences & Administration
10. **Further Mathematics** (`src/data/ss2FurtherMathData.ts`): Polynomials & remainder theorem, partial fractions, coordinate geometry of lines & circles, differentiation (product, quotient, chain rule), integration & area under curves, vectors & statics.
11. **Commerce** (`src/data/ss2CommerceData.ts`): Financial institutions & Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), stock exchange & capital market operations, insurance & risk management, international trade & balance of payments, modern business ownership & e-commerce.
12. **Government** (`src/data/ss2GovernmentData.ts`): Constitutionalism & rule of law, colonial administration (indirect rule), constitutional developments (Clifford 1922 to 1999), political parties, federalism & revenue allocation, foreign policy & ECOWAS/AU.

### Batch 5: Humanities & Physical Geography (Final Batch)
13. **Literature-in-English** (`src/data/ss2LiteratureData.ts`): African Drama (*The Lion and the Jewel* by Wole Soyinka), Non-African Drama (*Fences* by August Wilson), Poetic devices & diction, African Poetry (Niyi Osundare, Léopold Sédar Senghor, Lenrie Peters), Non-African Poetry (Dylan Thomas, T.S. Eliot), African Prose (*Second Class Citizen* by Buchi Emecheta), and WAEC essay clinic.
14. **Geography** (`src/data/ss2GeographyData.ts`): Fluvial processes & river landforms (upper/middle/lower course, ox-bow lakes, deltas), desert landforms (zeugens, yardangs, barchans), coastal processes (cave, arch, stack, stump, tombolo), glaciation (corries, arêtes, U-shaped valleys), atmospheric pressure & planetary wind belts, topographical contour mapwork & gradient, Köppen climate classification, world vegetation biomes, Nigerian relief, drainage, climate, and ecological vegetation zones.
15. **Christian Religious Studies (CRS)** (`src/data/ss2CRSData.ts`): Ministry of John the Baptist, wilderness temptation, call of the disciples & demands of discipleship, Sermon on the Mount (Beatitudes & Christian moral teachings), Synoptic miracles (nature, healing, resuscitation), parables of the Kingdom (Sower, Prodigal Son, Good Samaritan), Transfiguration, Triumphal Entry, Last Supper & Agony in Gethsemane, Sanhedrin and Roman trials, Crucifixion at Golgotha, Resurrection appearances, Great Commission, and WAEC two-part essay masterclass.

## Visual Presentation & Styling Standards
- **Strict Zero-ASCII Art Rule**: Every single diagram across all 15 subjects is rendered as a clean, responsive, dark-themed SVG visual component with glowing accents (emerald, cyan, amber, purple).
- **Structured Comparison Tables**: Comprehensive multi-column Markdown tables highlighting definitions, mechanisms, differences, formulas, and scriptural/scientific context.
- **Examination Grounding**: Every module concludes with 4 targeted Key Takeaways, WAEC/NECO/JAMB past exam problem walkthroughs, and a unique `associatedQuizId` linking to the CBT examination engine.
